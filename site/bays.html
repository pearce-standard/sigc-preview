02 / WAYS TO PLAY

Play a round. Work on your swing.

Choose how you want to spend your hour. Switch any time.

COURSE PLAY

Play the courses.

Tee it up on more than 550 courses from around the world, from a quick nine to a full eighteen with your group.

RANGE & PRACTICE

Know your numbers.

Hit balls on the range and see ball speed, launch angle and carry on every shot. It’s a good way to dial in a club before your next round.

GAMES

Make it a game.

Closest to the pin, long drive and other games keep everyone in it, whether they’re a scratch golfer or picking up a club for the first time.

03 / BRING YOUR PEOPLE

Room for the whole group.

Each bay fits up to five players, and three is the sweet spot for a steady pace. There’s leather seating behind the tee so nobody’s standing around waiting. One booking covers everyone in the bay.

  • Bring your own clubs, or rent a set for $10 (free for members)
  • BYOB welcome, with cooler, cup and coozie service
  • Indoor putting green and lounge between rounds

04 / MORE THAN THE BAYS

Stay awhile.

Roll a few putts on the indoor green, settle into the lounge, or catch the game with friends. The Standard is built for the time between shots, too.
