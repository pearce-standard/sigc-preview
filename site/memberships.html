05 / BEFORE AND AFTER HOURS

Play on your schedule.

  • Founding, Open and Player members can book bays from 5 AM to midnight in the booking app.
  • Outside staffed hours, members come in with a key card.
  • Key cards are handed out in person when staff is here. Stop by during staffed hours. We don’t mail them.
  • You still need a booking in the app, even after hours.
  • Non-members book during staffed hours.
