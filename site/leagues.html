01 / HOW IT WORKS

Play your round, your way.

Two-person teams, better ball, stroke net. Each of the nine weeks has its own course and its own round.

  1. 01Sign up.

    $60 per two-person team, or $30 as a single and we’ll pair you with a partner.

  2. 02Get your partner.

    Singles are paired by handicap so every team has a fair shot. You’ll get an email introduction before week 1.

  3. 03Play the week’s round.

    Each round runs Monday through Sunday. Book a bay any time that week, like any other visit.

  4. 04Climb the board.

    Your team counts the better net score on each hole. Standings update every week.

04 / SINGLES PAIRING

How we pair singles.

  • Singles sign-ups close a few days before week 1 (exact date TBD).
  • We sort singles by handicap, or average score if you don’t have one, and pair players so team handicaps land near the middle.
  • Your preferred night is the tie-breaker, so partners can actually play together.
  • Odd number of singles? We’ll work out a fair option with the last player.
  • You’ll get your partner’s name and first-week details by email.

06 / LEAGUE TERMS

The fine print.

Draft terms for the preview. Items marked TBD will be set before week 1.

Format

Two-person teams, better ball, stroke net, nine weekly rounds. Each round runs Monday through Sunday.

Entry and payment

$60 per two-person team or $30 per single. Signing up here doesn’t charge you. Payment is collected later through our booking system (YGB) or at the front desk. Bay time is booked and paid separately, like any visit. Members use their member time.

Handicaps

Use your handicap index, or your average 18-hole score if you don’t have one. The handicap source used for scoring (GHIN or TrackMan) is TBD.
