02 / HOW IT WORKS

Play when it suits you.

No set league night. Each round runs for a full week, and you book your own bay like any other visit.

  1. 01Sign up.

    $60 per two-person team, or Singles $30 and we’ll find you a partner.

  2. 02Play the week’s round.

    Each of the nine rounds is its own Monday–Sunday week. Play any time that week.

  3. 03Book your own bay.

    Members book their own bays as usual. No bays are held for the league.

  4. 04Better ball, stroke net.

    Your team counts the better net score on each hole. Results post to the standings.
