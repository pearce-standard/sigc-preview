01 / OUR STORY

Premium golf technology. Timeless hospitality.

The Standard brings together TrackMan iO bays, curated lounge seating, an indoor putting green and an easy, social atmosphere. It was inspired by the country’s best golf resorts and built to feel elevated yet approachable: refined but relaxed.

Whether you’re dialing in your swing, gathering with friends, hosting clients or playing in a league, this is your place to connect.

OUR MISSION

To create a space where great golf, great people, and great purpose come together.

OUR VISION

To offer a sanctuary of connection, where excellence is pursued, time slows down, and people gather around what matters.

For our families. For our community. For the Kingdom.

03 / A NOTE FROM OUR FOUNDER

Built with purpose.

“This idea started as a feeling, a memory from a country club locker room where I realized what belonging, presence, and aspiration looked like. That feeling stuck with me through years of golf trips, friendships, and a desire to one day create something that felt just as intentional. The Standard is that vision coming to life. It’s for my kids, my wife, my friends, and for everyone looking for something a little deeper than just a game.”

Eric Pearce, Founder
