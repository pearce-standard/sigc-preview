02 / BEFORE YOUR FIRST SWING

Come as you are.
Bring your game.

Courses, range and games.
All powered by TrackMan iO.

What should I bring?

Bring your own clubs, or rent a full set for $10. Rental clubs are free for members. BYOB is welcome, and we’ll keep your drinks cold with cooler, cup and coozie service.

Do I need to be a member?

No. Walk-ins and non-members are welcome during staffed hours. Members log in when booking to use their member time.

Are beginners and kids welcome?

Yes. Guests under 16 must be with an adult. Guests 16 and up can come on their own.

What can I play?

Full rounds on 550+ courses, range practice with shot data, and games. See the bays.
