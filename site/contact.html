02 / FAQ

Good to know
before you come.

Don’t see your question? Call (682) 477-5630.

Do I need a membership to play?

No. Walk-ins and non-members are welcome during staffed hours. Book a bay by the hour: $55 an hour Mon–Thu 9 AM–4 PM CT, $60 an hour at all other staffed hours, with a 1-hour minimum.

Can kids and teens come?

Yes. Guests under 16 must be with an adult. Guests 16 and older can come on their own.

How many people can play in one bay?

Up to five players, and we recommend three for a good pace. One booking covers the group.

Do I need my own clubs?

No. Bring yours, or rent a full set for $10. Rental clubs are free for members.

Can I bring drinks?

Yes. We’re BYOB, with cooler, cup and coozie service.

How far ahead can I book?

The public can book up to 10 days ahead. Members can book up to 14 days ahead.

Is there a booking fee?

A $0.50 reservation fee applies to each booking.

Can members play outside staffed hours?

Founding, Open and Player members can book from 5 AM to midnight and enter with a key card. Key cards are issued in person during staffed hours.

Do you host private events?

Yes. Group packages start at $216 for 2 bays for 2 hours. See events.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. The Ten-Play Gift Card is $299 and loads $600 in club credit. See gift cards.
